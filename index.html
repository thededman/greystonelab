Timex Sinclair.
Never stopped.

Free-building LEGO instead of following the instructions. Reading 2600 at the Wallingford library. Soldering RadioShack kits on the kitchen table. Same instinct, bigger toys.

Timex Sinclair 1000. 2KB of RAM and endless curiosity. Writing code before most kids could ride a bike.

'80s

RadioShack project books, a well-worn 2600 habit, and a home-built blue box. Lessons in how systems trust signals.
